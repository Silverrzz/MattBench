Position filtering & sampling

0 skips none; 1 skips everything and cannot train. Sampling happens while loading positions; source datasets are unchanged.

Ranges are inclusive. Ply counts half-moves from the game position; piece counts include kings. Tactical filtering skips recorded captures and promotions.

Keep probability for each piece count: 1 keeps all eligible positions, 0 drops them. These shape the distribution rather than specifying final proportions. Combined keep probability is (1 − random skip) × piece-count keep.
